# Legal Systems of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom does not have one uniform legal system. England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland have distinct legal jurisdictions, with differences in courts, procedure, and areas of law.

## Legislation and Devolution

The UK Parliament legislates on reserved matters, while devolved institutions can legislate within their areas of responsibility. This page is a general introduction, not a complete or current statement of the law; consult official sources for the relevant jurisdiction.
