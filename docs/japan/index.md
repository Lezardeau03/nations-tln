# Japan

**Official name:** Japan  
**Demonym:** Japanese  
**Capital:** Tokyo  
**Government:** Parliamentary constitutional monarchy

## Overview

Japan is an island country in East Asia. Its national government operates under a constitution that establishes a parliamentary system and defines the role of the Emperor as the symbol of the state.

Japanese is the principal language. The country includes large metropolitan areas as well as rural and island communities.
