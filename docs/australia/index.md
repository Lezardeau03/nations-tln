# Australia

**Official name:** Commonwealth of Australia  
**Demonym:** Australian  
**Capital:** Canberra  
**Government:** Federal parliamentary constitutional monarchy

## Overview

Australia is a country and continent in the Southern Hemisphere. Its federation comprises six states and two mainland territories, with additional external territories.

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples are the First Peoples of Australia and have diverse cultures and continuing connections to Country. English is the most widely used language.
