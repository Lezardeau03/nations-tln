# Government of Italy

Italy is a parliamentary republic established under the Constitution of the Italian Republic.

## National Institutions

- The President of the Republic is head of state.
- Parliament consists of the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Republic.
- The Government is led by the President of the Council of Ministers.
- The Constitutional Court reviews constitutional questions.

Italy is divided into regions, provinces or equivalent entities, and municipalities. Five regions have special autonomy arrangements.
