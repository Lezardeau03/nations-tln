# Government of Japan

Japan is a constitutional monarchy with a parliamentary government. The Constitution describes the Emperor as the symbol of the State, with no governing powers.

## National Institutions

- The National Diet is the legislature, comprising the House of Representatives and House of Councillors.
- The Prime Minister, designated by the Diet, leads the Cabinet.
- The Supreme Court is the highest court.

Japan is divided into 47 prefectures, each with local institutions.
