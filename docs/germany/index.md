# Germany

**Official name:** Federal Republic of Germany  
**Demonym:** German  
**Capital:** Berlin  
**Government:** Federal parliamentary republic

## Overview

Germany is a country in central Europe and a federal republic composed of sixteen states, known as Länder. Berlin is its capital, and German is the official language.

Its regions have distinct histories and landscapes, from North Sea coasts to the Alps.

See the dedicated pages for its government, geography, culture, and legal system.
