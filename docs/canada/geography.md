# Geography of Canada

Canada occupies much of northern North America and has coastlines on three oceans. It is the world's second-largest country by total area.

## Landscape

Its regions include the Arctic, Canadian Shield, prairie grasslands, Great Lakes–St. Lawrence lowlands, Pacific and Atlantic coasts, and the Rocky Mountains. Major waterways include the St. Lawrence River and Mackenzie River.

Climate varies from arctic in the north to temperate on parts of the southern coasts.
