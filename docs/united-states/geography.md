# Geography of the United States

The United States spans central North America, with Alaska in the northwest and Hawaii in the Pacific. It also administers territories in the Caribbean and Pacific.

## Landscape

Major regions include the Atlantic and Pacific coasts, the Appalachian and Rocky Mountains, the Great Plains, deserts of the southwest, and the Mississippi River basin. Alaska includes Arctic and subarctic areas; Hawaii is a volcanic island chain.

Climate ranges from tropical to arctic, with many temperate, continental, and arid regions.
