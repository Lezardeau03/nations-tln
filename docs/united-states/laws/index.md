# Legal System of the United States

The United States has a federal legal system. The Constitution, federal and state statutes, regulations, and court decisions all form part of the law, with authority depending on the subject and jurisdiction.

## Courts and Jurisdictions

Federal and state court systems operate alongside one another. Tribal nations also exercise governmental authority under their own legal systems and applicable federal law. This introduction is not a complete or current statement of the law; consult official sources for authoritative information.
