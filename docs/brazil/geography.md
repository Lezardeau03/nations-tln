# Geography of Brazil

Brazil covers a large portion of eastern and central South America and borders every South American country except Chile and Ecuador. It has a long Atlantic coastline.

## Landscape

The Amazon Basin occupies much of the north. Other major regions include the Brazilian Highlands, the Pantanal wetlands, the Cerrado savanna, and the Atlantic Forest. The Amazon is the country's largest river system.

Climate is mostly tropical, with regional differences including semi-arid conditions in parts of the northeast and subtropical conditions in the south.
