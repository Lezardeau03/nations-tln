# Government of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is a constitutional monarchy and parliamentary democracy. The monarch is head of state, while the Prime Minister leads the government.

## National and Devolved Institutions

- The UK Parliament at Westminster consists of the House of Commons and House of Lords.
- The UK Government is accountable to Parliament.
- Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland have devolved legislatures or assemblies and governments with powers defined by law.

The arrangements differ among the four constituent countries, and not all policy areas are devolved.
