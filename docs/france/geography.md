# Geography of France

Metropolitan France occupies the western part of Europe and includes both mainland territory and the island of Corsica. Overseas France includes regions and territories in the Atlantic, Indian, and Pacific oceans, as well as South America.

## Landscape

The mainland includes Atlantic and Mediterranean coasts, river basins, plains, and mountain ranges such as the Alps, Pyrenees, and Massif Central. The Loire, Rhône, Seine, and Garonne are among its major rivers.

France's climates range from oceanic in the west to Mediterranean in the south and alpine in high mountain areas.
