# Geography of China

China is in East Asia and borders fourteen countries. Its territory stretches from the Pacific coast in the east to high plateaus and mountains in the west.

## Landscape

Major features include the Himalayas and Tibetan Plateau, the Gobi Desert, broad river plains, and coastal lowlands. The Yangtze and Yellow rivers are among the country's major waterways.

Climate varies widely, from arid and high-altitude regions to humid subtropical areas in the south and monsoon-influenced eastern regions.
