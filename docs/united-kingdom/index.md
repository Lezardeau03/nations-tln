# United Kingdom

**Official name:** United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland  
**Demonym:** British  
**Capital:** London  
**Government:** Parliamentary constitutional monarchy

## Overview

The United Kingdom is a state in northwestern Europe comprising England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. Each constituent country has a distinct identity; Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland have devolved institutions.

London is the capital. English is the principal language, and Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Irish, and other languages are also spoken.

See the dedicated pages for its government, geography, culture, and legal system.
