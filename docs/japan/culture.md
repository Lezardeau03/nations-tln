# Culture of Japan

Japanese culture includes traditions that developed across distinct regions and periods, as well as contemporary forms of art, design, literature, food, and popular media. Japanese is the principal language, with regional varieties and minority languages also present.

## Traditions and Contemporary Life

Seasonal festivals, local crafts, religious practices, and regional cuisines form part of community life. Modern Japanese culture also has an international presence in animation, games, fashion, and music. Customs vary among individuals and communities.
