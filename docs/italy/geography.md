# Geography of Italy

Italy occupies a peninsula in the Mediterranean and includes the islands of Sicily and Sardinia. It borders France, Switzerland, Austria, and Slovenia, and encloses the independent states of San Marino and Vatican City.

## Landscape

The Alps form much of the northern boundary, while the Apennines extend along the peninsula. The Po Valley is a major lowland region. Italy also has active volcanoes, including Etna and Vesuvius.

The climate ranges from alpine in the north to Mediterranean in many coastal and southern areas.
