# Legal System of Canada

Canada's legal system includes federal and provincial or territorial law. It combines common-law traditions with Quebec's civil-law system for private law, alongside Indigenous legal orders and rights.

## Courts and Jurisdiction

The Constitution allocates powers among federal, provincial, and territorial institutions. The Supreme Court of Canada is the final court of appeal. This page is a general introduction, not a complete or current statement of the law; consult official sources for the relevant jurisdiction.
