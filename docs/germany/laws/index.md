# Legal System of Germany

Germany has a civil-law legal system based on the Basic Law, federal and state legislation, regulations, and court decisions. Federal and Länder responsibilities are defined by the constitutional framework.

## Courts

Separate federal courts serve different areas of jurisdiction, alongside courts operated by the Länder. The Federal Constitutional Court rules on constitutional questions. This page is a general introduction, not a complete or current statement of the law; consult official sources for authoritative information.
