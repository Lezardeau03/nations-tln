# Italy

**Official name:** Italian Republic  
**Demonym:** Italian  
**Capital:** Rome  
**Government:** Parliamentary republic

## Overview

Italy is a country in southern Europe, extending into the Mediterranean Sea. Its territory includes the mainland peninsula, Sicily, Sardinia, and smaller islands.

Rome is the capital, and Italian is the official language. The country includes regions with distinct histories, languages, and traditions.
