# France

**Official name:** French Republic  
**Demonym:** French  
**Capital:** Paris  
**Government:** Semi-presidential republic

## Overview

France is a country in western Europe with overseas regions and territories in other parts of the world. Its national institutions coexist with strong local identities and a varied cultural life.

Paris is the capital and largest city. France is a member of the European Union and has French as its official language.

See the dedicated pages for its government, geography, culture, and legal system.
