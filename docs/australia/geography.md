# Geography of Australia

Australia occupies the Australian continent and nearby islands, including Tasmania. Its mainland is bordered by the Indian and Pacific oceans.

## Landscape

The continent includes arid and semi-arid interior regions, tropical areas in the north, temperate southeast and southwest coasts, and extensive river basins. The Great Dividing Range runs along much of the eastern side.

The climate ranges from tropical in the north to temperate in the south, with large dry regions inland.
