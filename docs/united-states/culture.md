# Culture of the United States

The United States is culturally and linguistically diverse, shaped by Indigenous peoples, immigration, regional histories, and global exchange. English is widely used, and Spanish and many other languages are spoken across the country.

## Arts and Traditions

Music, literature, film, food, and sports reflect a broad range of local and community traditions. Indigenous nations maintain distinct cultures and governments. National observances include Independence Day on 4 July, alongside numerous state and community celebrations.
