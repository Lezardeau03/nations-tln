# Government of Canada

Canada is a federal parliamentary democracy and constitutional monarchy. The monarch is head of state and is represented federally by the Governor General.

## Institutions and Federalism

- The federal Parliament comprises the Crown, the Senate, and the House of Commons.
- The Prime Minister leads the federal government.
- The Supreme Court of Canada is the country's highest court.
- Provinces and territories have their own legislatures and governments, with powers set out in the Constitution.

Indigenous governments and institutions have varied forms and relationships with federal, provincial, and territorial governments.
