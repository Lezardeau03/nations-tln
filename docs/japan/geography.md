# Geography of Japan

Japan is an archipelago in the Pacific Ocean, east of the Korean Peninsula and China. Its four principal islands are Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu; many smaller islands extend across a broad north–south range.

## Landscape

Mountains cover much of the country, and settlements are concentrated in coastal plains. Japan has active volcanoes, including Mount Fuji, and experiences earthquakes and seasonal weather systems.

The climate ranges from cool and snowy in the north to subtropical in parts of the south.
