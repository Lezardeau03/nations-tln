# United States

**Official name:** United States of America  
**Demonym:** American  
**Capital:** Washington, D.C.  
**Government:** Federal presidential constitutional republic

## Overview

The United States is a federal country in North America consisting of fifty states, a federal district, and territories. Its large territory includes many climates, landscapes, and communities.

Washington, D.C. is the national capital. English is the most widely used language; the country has no federally designated official language.

See the dedicated pages for its government, geography, culture, and legal system.
