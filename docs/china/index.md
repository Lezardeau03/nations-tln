# China

**Official name:** People's Republic of China  
**Demonym:** Chinese  
**Capital:** Beijing  
**Government:** Socialist republic

## Overview

The People's Republic of China occupies a large area of East Asia and includes a wide range of regions, environments, and communities. Beijing is the capital, and Mandarin Chinese is the country's official language.

China has a long and varied history and is home to many ethnic groups and regional cultures.

See the dedicated pages for its government, geography, culture, and legal system.
