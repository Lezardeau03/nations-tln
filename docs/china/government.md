# Government of China

The People's Republic of China is governed under its Constitution and led by the Communist Party of China. The National People's Congress is the highest state organ under the constitutional structure.

## State Institutions

- The National People's Congress and its Standing Committee exercise legislative functions.
- The President is the head of state.
- The State Council is the central people's government and executive body.
- The Supreme People's Court is the highest judicial organ.

The country has provincial-level, prefectural, county, and township-level administrative divisions. Hong Kong and Macao have special administrative region status.
