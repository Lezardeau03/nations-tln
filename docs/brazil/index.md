# Brazil

**Official name:** Federative Republic of Brazil  
**Demonym:** Brazilian  
**Capital:** Brasília  
**Government:** Federal presidential republic

## Overview

Brazil is the largest country in South America and includes extensive Amazonian territory, major river systems, and densely populated coastal regions. Brasília is the capital, and Portuguese is the official language.

Its regions are home to diverse communities and cultural traditions.

See the dedicated pages for its government, geography, culture, and legal system.
