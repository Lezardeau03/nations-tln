# Government of Germany

Germany is a federal parliamentary republic governed under the Basic Law.

## Federal Institutions

- The Bundestag is the federal parliament elected by the public.
- The Bundesrat represents the sixteen Länder in federal matters.
- The Federal Chancellor heads the Federal Government.
- The Federal President is the head of state.
- The Federal Constitutional Court reviews constitutional matters.

The Länder have their own constitutions, parliaments, and governments and participate in the federal system.
