# Culture of the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom's cultural life reflects the histories and identities of England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, as well as migration and global exchange. English is widely spoken; Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Irish, Scots, and other languages are part of the country's linguistic landscape.

## Arts and Traditions

Literature, music, theatre, sport, and regional festivals all contribute to cultural life. Traditions differ across communities, and contemporary culture continues to evolve.
