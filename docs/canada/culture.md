# Culture of Canada

Canada's cultural life reflects Indigenous peoples, English- and French-speaking communities, immigration, and regional histories. English and French are federal official languages; many Indigenous and other languages are spoken across the country.

## Communities and Traditions

First Nations, Inuit, and Métis peoples have distinct cultures and identities. Regional arts, food, festivals, and languages vary across provinces and territories. This overview recognizes diversity rather than treating Canadian culture as uniform.
