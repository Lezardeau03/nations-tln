# Government of Australia

Australia is a federal parliamentary democracy and constitutional monarchy. The Constitution establishes the Commonwealth and distributes powers between it and the states.

## National Institutions

- The monarch is head of state and is represented federally by the Governor-General.
- The Commonwealth Parliament comprises the Crown, the Senate, and the House of Representatives.
- The Prime Minister leads the government.
- The High Court of Australia is the highest court.

States and territories have their own institutions and differing constitutional arrangements.
