# Canada

**Official name:** Canada  
**Demonym:** Canadian  
**Capital:** Ottawa  
**Government:** Federal parliamentary constitutional monarchy

## Overview

Canada is a federal country in North America, comprising ten provinces and three territories. It has coastlines on the Atlantic, Pacific, and Arctic oceans.

English and French are the official languages of the federal government. Indigenous peoples, including First Nations, Inuit, and Métis, have distinct histories and communities across the country.
