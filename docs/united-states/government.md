# Government of the United States

The United States is a constitutional federal republic. The Constitution establishes a national government with powers divided among three branches and shared with the states.

## Federal Institutions

- The President leads the executive branch and serves as head of state and government.
- Congress, made up of the House of Representatives and Senate, exercises federal legislative power.
- The Supreme Court heads the federal judiciary.

Each state has its own government. The federal district and territories have distinct arrangements.
