# Culture of France

French is the national language, alongside regional languages and languages spoken by communities throughout the country. French cultural life includes literature, visual arts, film, music, fashion, and cuisine.

## Regional Traditions

Regions have distinct histories, foods, festivals, and architectural traditions. Cultural heritage is shaped by both metropolitan and overseas communities and by centuries of exchange with other parts of the world.

National celebrations include Bastille Day on 14 July. This overview is not intended to imply that all people in France share the same customs or beliefs.
